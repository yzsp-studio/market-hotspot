## 🇨🇳 中文

把宏观、政策、监管、供需、地缘、行业或公司事件转化为公司、行业与公开市场的因果影响分析。它覆盖事件状态、基线、传导渠道、财务与估值影响、直接及高阶影响、priced-in 判断、情景、监控与证伪，适合做事件影响、政策冲击、跨资产传导和公司事件研究。它明确不用于没有明确事件的公司基本面分析或普通新闻摘要。输出受硬模板约束：开头固定为不超过 5 行的可审计摘要；精确数字逐条带来源标记；关键输入缺失时改写成分档表，不假设也不拒答；没有一手来源的量级只能进假设卡。

### ✨ 能做什么

- 事件 → 公司 / 行业 / 公开市场三层因果影响分析
- 拆解基线、传导渠道，并测算财务与估值影响
- 直接效应与高阶影响分开列，避免只讲第一层
- 给出 priced-in 判断、情景分析、监控指标与证伪条件
- 精确数字逐条带来源标记，开头固定为 ≤5 行可审计摘要
- 关键输入缺失时改写成分档表，无一手来源的量级只进假设卡，不假设也不拒答

## 🇬🇧 English

Turns a macro, policy, regulatory, supply-demand, geopolitical, industry, or company event into a causal impact analysis across the company, its industry, and public markets. It covers event state, baseline, transmission channels, financial and valuation impact, direct and higher-order effects, a priced-in judgement, scenarios, monitoring, and falsification — suited to event impact, policy shock, cross-asset transmission, and company event research. It is explicitly not for company fundamentals without a clear event, nor for plain news summaries. Output is bound to a hard template: the opening is an auditable summary of at most five lines, precise numbers carry per-item source markers, missing key inputs become a tiered table instead of assumptions or a refusal, and magnitudes without a primary source may only enter an assumption card.

### ✨ What it does

- Event to causal impact analysis across company, industry, and public markets
- Breaks down baseline and transmission channels, then sizes financial and valuation impact
- Lists direct effects separately from higher-order effects instead of stopping at layer one
- Delivers a priced-in judgement, scenarios, monitoring indicators, and falsification conditions
- Precise numbers carry per-item source markers; the opening is an auditable summary of at most five lines
- Missing key inputs become a tiered table and source-less magnitudes go only into an assumption card — never assumptions, never a refusal
